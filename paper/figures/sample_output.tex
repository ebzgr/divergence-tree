    \caption{\label{figure:sample}
Divergence tree segmentation results from synthetic free-trial subscription data. 
The splitting variables $X[1]$, $X[2]$, and $X[0]$ correspond to the underlying 
constructs of novelty, sunk cost, and usefulness, respectively. Higher values 
indicate stronger presence of the construct. Each node reports estimated treatment 
effects for the firm outcome ($\tau_F$) and consumer outcome ($\tau_C$). Leaf nodes 
are color-coded by outcome alignment: green (win–win, $\tau_F>0,\tau_C>0$), gray 
(lose–lose), red (firm gains but consumer loses), and blue (consumer gains but 
firm loses). The tree highlights interpretable segments where firm and consumer 
interests either converge or diverge.
}
